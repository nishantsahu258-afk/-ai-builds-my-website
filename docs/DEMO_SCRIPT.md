# 5-Minute Demo Script

## Business 1: Bro Code Cafe (Manual-Only & Source Relevance Demo)
1. **Create Project:** Enter `Bro Code Cafe`, `Restaurant & Cafe`, `Durg`, `Chhattisgarh`, `India`.
2. **Upload Sources:** Upload a matching cafe PDF (or attach an unrelated link to demonstrate relevance flagging).
3. **Verify Screen:** Show how mismatched/failed sources are flagged and excluded without blocking **Generate with Manual Facts Only**.
4. **Editor & Publish:** Inspect that only confirmed manual facts appear (missing fields show editable placeholders), then publish and open the public URL.

## Business 2: Local Retail / Service Business (Full Verified Flow)
1. **Create Project:** Enter business name, category, and city (e.g., `Lauda Shop`).
2. **Extract & Confirm:** Upload a matching brochure PDF, confirm extracted facts on the Verification screen, and resolve any conflicts.
3. **Generate & Edit:** Run normal AI generation, tweak a section in the Editor, and publish Version 1.
4. **Public Renderer:** Open the live published page (e.g. `https://client-murex-five-31.vercel.app/sites/lauda-shop-60cbed`) and verify it renders the immutable published snapshot.
